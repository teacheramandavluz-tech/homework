Listening — A Lesson I Learned

Listen twice before opening the transcript. Take notes instead of trying to understand every word.

Listens: 0 / 2

The activity will choose a British English voice whenever your browser provides one.

1. What did the speaker fail when she was fourteen?

2. Why did she hide the paper?

3. What has she learned about asking for help?

4. How has she changed since that day?

5. Write one Present Perfect structure and one Past Simple verb you heard.

Transcript

School has taught me more than facts. I have learned that asking for help is a strength. When I was fourteen, I failed an important maths test. I felt embarrassed, so I hid the paper in my backpack. A teacher noticed that I was unusually quiet and asked me what had happened. Since that day, I have become more honest about what I don’t understand. I have also stopped pretending that mistakes are failures. That experience taught me to ask questions, practise patiently, and try again.
